# E-Commerce-Executive-Overview-Dashboard
# 📊 E-Commerce Executive Overview Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce business performance through an interactive **Executive Overview Dashboard** developed as part of my **Data Analysis Internship at Primeor Solutions**.

The objective was to transform business data into meaningful insights by tracking key performance indicators (KPIs), analyzing sales and profitability trends, and presenting business performance through interactive visualizations.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor overall e-commerce business performance.
- Analyze sales, profit, orders, and average discount.
- Evaluate year-wise sales and profit trends.
- Understand category-wise sales distribution.
- Analyze market-wise business performance.
- Enable dynamic data exploration through interactive filters.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Interactive dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Business Intelligence** – Business performance monitoring.
- **Data Analysis** – KPI evaluation and trend analysis.
- **Data Visualization** – Visual representation of business metrics.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks the following business metrics:

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | Overall sales performance |
| Total Profit | Overall business profitability |
| Total Orders | Total number of orders |
| Average Discount | Average discount offered |

## 🔍 Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Sales & Profit Analysis
Analyzed overall sales and profit performance to understand business growth and profitability.

### 2. Year-wise Performance Analysis
Evaluated yearly sales and profit trends to understand changes in business performance over time.

### 3. Category-wise Sales Distribution
Analyzed sales contributions across different product categories.

### 4. Market Performance Analysis
Explored market-level performance to understand sales and profitability across different markets.

### 5. Interactive Dashboard & Filters
Implemented interactive filters to enable dynamic data exploration and performance analysis.

## 💡 Business Value

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of e-commerce performance and helps:
- Monitor important business KPIs.
- Identify sales and profitability trends.
- Compare category and market performance.
- Support informed, data-driven business decisions.
